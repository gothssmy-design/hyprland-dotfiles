✦ HIGHLIGHTS

🖥️ Dual 120Hz Monitor Setup

Independent workspace bars for laptop screen (workspaces 1-5) and external 27" 120Hz Philips monitor (workspaces 6-10).

⚡ Auto Power Switching

Background daemon switches CPU/GPU to performance mode on AC power, and downclocks to power-saver mode on battery.

🎞️ Freeze-Free Resume

Custom direct-scanout and DPMS wake handlers prevent screen freezes when resuming from sleep mode on AMD GPUs.

🎨 Vintage Monochrome Style

Crisp 1px borders, #121212 dark charcoal palette, Tokyo Night terminal theme, and sharp box geometry.
